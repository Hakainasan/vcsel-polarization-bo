# Audit trail (five rounds)

Scope: the original README + notebook + requirements supplied by the author (round 1), and the first rebuilt version of this
repository (round 2, after the author asked whether the literature had been checked, whether the citations were real, and which
aperture is actually best).  Everything computed here is a **model prediction**; the audit is about scientific validity first.
Round 3 is the pass over the complete results; Round 4 records what three independent reviews of the finished package found and what was changed; Round 5 (first section below) is a short consistency check of the application texts against the author's own statements.

## Limitations that remain

Single-mode spin-flip model for apertures that may be multimode; scalar mode solver used only as a weight; phenomenological linear coupling between boundary tensors and anisotropy;
oxidation depth and anisotropy are placeholders; the sizes of the fabricated apertures and the number of devices per design are not in the text that was read;
the corner treatment of mask family A is assumed sharp; thresholds that translate "most / seldom / part of the devices" into numbers are choices (loose and strict variants are reported);
nothing has been checked against a measurement from the author's own devices.
Round 3 adds: yields presume switching from spin-flip dynamics at fixed anisotropy (thermal drift would invalidate them); the oxide anisotropy decides whether the model-selected batch beats the author's mask.
Round 4 adds: the headline best-of-batch yields are optimistic (0.86 on 32 test worlds, 0.74 on 117 further accepted worlds); the optimiser benchmark rests on five simulated wafers and eight P3 runs; the published designs are drawn at assumed sizes.
